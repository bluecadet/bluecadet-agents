# Known Figures — Primary Source Sets

Confirmed primary-source site sets for figures already verified with this skill. Add a new entry here whenever a good source set gets nailed down for someone new, so the next person doesn't have to rediscover it from scratch.

## Harry S. Truman

**Fast-path sources:**

- `site:presidency.ucsb.edu` — The American Presidency Project. Covers the presidency only (1945-1953: speeches, messages, statements, press conferences, proclamations, executive orders — roughly the same content as *Public Papers of the Presidents*), but fully text-searchable.
- `site:trumanlibrary.gov` — the Truman Library's own digitized collections, including their own mirror of the Public Papers (`/library/public-papers/...`) plus other online collections. Broader coverage than the presidency alone, but not everything has a text layer — some collections are scan-only images with no OCR, so a search can miss something that's technically hosted there.

**Known gaps:** pre-presidential material (pre-1945: Senate speeches, personal letters), personal/unofficial material (letters to Bess, diary entries, oral histories), and post-presidential material (memoirs, later lectures like the 1959 Columbia lectures) aren't covered by American Presidency Project at all, and may or may not be searchable at trumanlibrary.gov depending on OCR status. A clean miss on both doesn't rule these out, it means falling through to the scanned-PDF workflow.

**Scanned-PDF fallback source:** *Public Papers of the Presidents of the United States: Harry S. Truman* (bound volumes, GPO). Chronological index near the front of each volume (item number, title, date, printed page number).

## Adding a new figure

When verifying quotes for a figure not listed here, before running the fast-path search:

1. Identify the figure's own institutional archive(s) — a presidential library, a national archive, a foundation, university special collections, etc.
2. Note which of those archives are actually text-searchable (has a real search index) versus scan-only/image-only.
3. Once confirmed working, add an entry here in the same format as Truman's, including known coverage gaps, so the next verification run (by anyone) starts from a known-good source set instead of rediscovering it.
